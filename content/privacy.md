# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 January 2026_

HakimeRad Teleradiology Consultancy PLC ("HakimeRAD", "we", "us") provides
teleradiology services to healthcare institutions across Ethiopia. This Privacy
Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use
**hakimerad.health.et** and our related services.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Information you provide**: name, email, phone, organisation, and message
  content when you fill out our contact form or correspond with us.
- **Imaging data** (when you become a customer): DICOM studies and reports
  submitted through our PACS/RIS platform under a separate Data Processing
  Agreement.
- **Technical information**: standard server logs (IP address, user-agent,
  pages viewed) used for security and analytics.

## 2. How we use your information

- To respond to inquiries and provide the services you request.
- To deliver radiology reports and operate the platform under contract.
- To improve our services and ensure platform security.
- To comply with applicable law.

We do **not** sell your information.

## 3. Data security

All imaging data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
Access is gated by role-based access control, and every administrative action
is logged in tamper-evident audit logs.

## 4. Data residency

We store production imaging data within Ethiopia-aligned cloud regions
wherever feasible. Where cross-border processing is required (for example,
sub-specialist review), we use only providers under appropriate contractual
safeguards.

## 5. Sharing

We share information only with: (a) the radiologists who interpret the
studies; (b) service providers that help us operate the platform (under
confidentiality and security obligations); and (c) authorities when required
by law.

## 6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal
information by emailing us at **info@hakimerad.health.et**.

## 7. Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies (e.g. to remember that you accepted
this notice). We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced on this page
with a new "Last updated" date.

**+251 906 373 154**.
