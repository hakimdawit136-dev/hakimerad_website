About HakimeRAD

A new vision for radiology in Ethiopia.

Our Story

HakimeRad Teleradiology Consultancy PLC is dedicated to bridging the diagnostic service delivery gap in Ethiopia through secure, AI-supported, and cloud-enabled teleradiology platforms. Founded by Dr. Dawit Muluneh, we connect healthcare institutions with certified radiologists to ensure timely and accurate interpretations for X-ray, CT, MRI, Ultrasound, and more.

Our focus is on underserved areas, including public hospitals, private clinics, and rural zones, to make diagnostic imaging accessible and affordable across the nation and the Horn of Africa.
